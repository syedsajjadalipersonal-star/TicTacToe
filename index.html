How to play
  1. Win three small boards in a row (across, down or diagonal) to win the game.
  2. Where you place a mark decides which small board your opponent must play in next. A mark in the top-right square of any board sends them to the top-right board.
  3. Three in a row on a small board wins it and locks it with a big X or O.
  4. If you are sent to a board that is already won or full, you get a wildcard: play any open square on any open board.
  5. X goes first, and may play anywhere.
